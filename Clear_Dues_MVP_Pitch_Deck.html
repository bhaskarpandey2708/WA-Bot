Add-On to Existing Operations

Clear Dues does not replace CRM/LMS/LOS/ERP. It integrates as an operational layer on top of your existing stack.

Integration Modes

  • File-based intake (CSV/XLSX/JSON) from current systems
  • API-driven campaign trigger and status updates
  • Webhook-based inbound conversation capture
  • Payment link insertion for instant borrower action

Operating Outcomes

  • Faster reminder cycles and reduced manual follow-up
  • Centralized conversation and disposition visibility
  • Lead assignment to junior/senior agents for closure
  • Cost control with inbound disconnect switch
